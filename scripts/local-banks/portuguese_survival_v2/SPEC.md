# Portuguese Survival v2 — Day-1 speaking moves

**Not** a CIPLE subset. Own bank of dialogue moves for living in Portugal (PT-PT).

## Product

- Gumroad slug (reuse): `portuguese-survival-anki-deck`
- Target size: **380–420** unique moves (not 1000 lemmas)
- Promise: open your mouth on day 1 — shops, clinic, housing, transport, docs

## Card types

| type | Front | Back | Audio |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| `say` | EN scene + what to do | 1–2 PT replies + hint | primary reply |
| `hear` | EN cue + “what did they say?” (optional audio of their line) | PT line + short EN gloss + hint | their line |
| `repair` | EN stuck moment | PT repair line(s) + hint | repair line |

Ratio target: ~60% say / 25% hear / 15% repair.

## Scenes

`foundations` · `numbers_money_time` · `directions` · `shop` · `health` · `housing` · `transport` · `docs` · `social`

## Rules

- European Portuguese only (tu rare with strangers; prefer você)
- obrigado / obrigada — note gender when relevant
- No CIPLE exam trivia, politics, abstract nouns
- Every card must unblock a real day-1 moment
- Hint ≥ 40 chars: trap, register, or next move
- `audio_text` = exact string to TTS (pt-PT)

## IDs

`{scene}-{type}-{nn}` e.g. `shop-say-03`
