Which German A2 pathways does this cover?

Goethe-Institut A2, telc Deutsch A2, and ÖSD Zertifikat A2 are widely recognized certificates. DTZ is the immigrant integration exam used in Germany. The same A2 lexicon supports residence / Einbürgerung language and fide / Swiss residency German. For Leben in Deutschland civics use the Citizenship & Naturalization Anki Bundle; for Swiss Staatskunde use the Swiss Citizenship Anki Bundle.
