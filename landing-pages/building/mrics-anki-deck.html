Exam & certification

What is the RICS APC / MRICS?

MRICS membership is awarded through the RICS Assessment of Professional Competence (APC). Candidates demonstrate mandatory and technical competencies, ethics, and usually a case study plus final assessment interview — not a single multiple-choice licensure exam.
