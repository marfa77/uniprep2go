What is BMS / BAS prep for?

Building Management / Automation Systems control HVAC, lighting, and plant equipment. There is no single U.S. federal BMS license — careers typically combine BACnet knowledge, vendor platform training (e.g. Niagara / manufacturer certs), and field commissioning.
