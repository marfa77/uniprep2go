## 2. [Patient Safety and Quality Assurance] Suffix confusion  score=100.7
Q: Why do drug name suffixes such as XL, SR, ER, and DR cause errors?
A: Suffixes change release rate and dosing frequency: Wellbutrin SR (twice daily) vs XL (once daily); Depakote DR vs Depakote ER; Tegretol vs Tegretol XR. Omitting or swapping a suffix can double or halve the effective dose. ISMP warns about inconsistent suffix meanings.

Miss: Treating any extended-release suffix as interchangeable.
